Elena stood on the observation deck, watching the rain weep against the reinforced glass, mirroring the deep sorrow in her heart. Her throat tightened as a cold sweat broke out across her neck. Her heart hammered against her ribs, skipping a beat with every chime of the docking terminal. 

The air wafted with the smell of ozone and damp copper, a metallic tang that tasted of dread. She clutched her datapad with trembling hands, knuckles white as she remembered the disaster. A knot of dread formed in the pit of her stomach.

Her breath caught in her chest when Marcus stepped into the corridor. 

"Is it true?" Marcus asked, his voice thick with unvoiced agony. "Did we sacrifice everything only to discover the stars were never listening?"

"Perhaps listening was never the destination," Elena replied, looking past him into the infinite expanse. 

In that moment, she understood what it truly meant to let go. The journey had not been about finding another home among distant galaxies, but about finding the quiet peace settled within herself, preserving the fragile memory in amber for generations yet unborn.
