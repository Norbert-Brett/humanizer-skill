# GitHub Copilot Instructions: Humanizer 2.0 Anti-Slop Directive

When composing or refining narrative prose, creative fiction, essays, or character dialogue:

- **Kill Moralizing Epilogues**: Do not conclude stories with characters reflecting on what was "truly learned" or finding neat internal peace.
- **Eliminate Somatic Reflex Clichés**: Replace "throat tightened", "heart hammered against ribs", "cold sweat broke out", and "breath caught" with direct emotion labels ("she was terrified", "he felt exhausted").
- **Dismantle Pathetic Fallacy**: Keep weather and settings emotionally neutral, indifferent, or realistically mismatched to character interiority.
- **Break Linear Continuity**: Interleave anachrony, flashbacks, and contrasting subplots. Let institutional friction or accidents shape climax outcomes.
- **Concrete Grounding**: Anchor scenes with real entity names, specific locations, technical objects, and dates.
